# My Portfolio Website

## Project overview

I built this site to introduce myself, show my IT skills and projects, and give people an easy way to contact me.

## Target audience

Employers, recruiters, classmates, and anyone who wants to see what I work on.

## Part 1: Content

1. **What is my full name?**  
   Faisal Shehzad.

2. **Why did I make this website?**  
   I wanted my background, skills, and projects in one place.

3. **Who do I want to visit my website?**  
   Mainly employers and recruiters, but also classmates and other people in tech.

4. **What skills do I want to show?**  
   IT support, Windows and systems, networking, and hardware.

5. **What work will I share?**  
   My home IT lab, electronics and device repair, and a car automation project.

6. **How would I describe myself?**  
   I'm a Computer Programming and Information Systems student at Farmingdale State College and a Field Technology Technician. I like fixing technology problems and helping people with them.

7. **What pages did I include?**  
   Introduction, Skills, Projects, and Contact.

8. **What is my career goal?**  
   I want to work in IT support and keep learning as I go.

9. **What tools have I used?**  
   Windows, Linux, Macrium Reflect, Microsoft Office, and basic Python and SQL.

10. **What experience or achievements will I highlight?**  
    My job as a Field Technology Technician, where I help people with computers, printers, and network problems, and the hands-on projects I do on my own time.

11. **What do I want visitors to do?**  
    Look through my projects or contact me about an opportunity.

12. **Will I include my resume?**  
    No, not on this site.

13. **What links will I include?**  
    My email for now. I might add GitHub or LinkedIn later.

## Part 2: Design

1. **What style did I choose?**  
   Simple and professional, with a few personal touches like my photo.

2. **What colors do I use, and why?**  
   A cream background, dark green text, and orange for links and small accents. They feel calm and are easy to read.

3. **What fonts do I use?**  
   Georgia for headings and Arial for body text.

4. **How does the design reflect me?**  
   It's plain and practical, which is how I try to work through IT problems.

5. **What is the homepage layout?**  
   A menu at the top, then my photo and heading, two short paragraphs about me, two short sections called "What I'm doing" and "What matters to me," and a link to my projects.

6. **How do I show my projects?**  
   Each project gets its own row, with the title on the left and a short description on the right.

7. **Will my site work on phones?**  
   Partly. On screens under 600px wide, my photo gets smaller and moves above my heading. The rest of the layout stays the same.

8. **How do I guide visitors?**  
   The same menu is on every page, each page has a clear heading, and there's a link at the bottom pointing to the next logical page.

9. **How do I keep the pages consistent?**  
   Every page uses the same menu, colors, fonts, and footer.

10. **How do I make the site easier to read?**  
    Simple text, readable sizes, plain links, and a dark mode button that remembers your choice.

11. **Do I use images or icons?**  
    Just my own photo on the Introduction page. The other pages are text.

12. **What websites inspired me?**  
    I looked at a few portfolio sites for general ideas, but I didn't copy any of them.
