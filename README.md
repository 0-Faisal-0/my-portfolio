# My Portfolio Website

## Project overview

I made this website to introduce myself, share my IT skills and projects, and make it easy for people to contact me.

## Target audience

I made this site for employers, recruiters, classmates, and people who want to learn about my work.

## Part 2: Design

1. **What style did I choose?**  
   I chose a simple, professional style with a personal touch.

2. **What colors do I use, and why?**  
   I use a warm cream background, dark green text, and orange details. I chose them because they feel calm and are easy to read.

3. **What fonts do I use?**  
   I use Georgia for headings and Arial for regular text.

4. **How does the design reflect me?**  
   I kept the pages clear and practical, which fits how I approach IT problems.

5. **What is the homepage layout?**  
   My homepage has a menu at the top, my introduction and photo, a short background section, and a link to my projects.

6. **How do I show my projects?**  
   I show my projects in a numbered list with a short description of each one.

7. **Will my site work on phones?**  
   Yes. I use a CSS rule to adjust the layout for smaller screens.

8. **How do I guide visitors?**  
   I use clear headings, a simple menu, and links beside related information.

9. **How do I keep the pages consistent?**  
   I use the same menu, colors, fonts, and footer on each page.

10. **How do I make the site easier to read?**  
    I use clear text, readable sizes, good color contrast, and simple links.

11. **Do I use images or icons?**  
    I include my own photo on the Introduction page. The other pages mainly use text.

12. **What websites inspired me?**  
    I looked at general professional portfolio ideas, but I did not copy one specific website.
